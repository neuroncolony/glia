Privacy

What we store

Your wallet address, a signed in session tied to that address, chat history linked to your wallet, API keys you create, and launch and trade records that are already public on chain.

What we do not collect

No email, no name, no phone number, and no tracking cookies. The browser keeps a few localStorage values for your theme, your session, and a first visit flag. That is all.

Third parties

OpenRouter runs model inference and sees the prompts you send. The Robinhood Chain RPC sees the addresses you query. Railway hosts the server. Each sees the minimum needed to do its job.

Retention and deletion

Chat history and API keys can be deleted from the site while you are signed in. For full removal of everything tied to your address, email hello@glia.fun from a channel you control.
