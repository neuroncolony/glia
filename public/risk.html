Risk disclosure

Speculative assets

Bonding curve tokens are highly speculative. They can lose all of their value, and most of them do. Only use money you can afford to lose entirely.

Unaudited contracts

The pons v2 contracts and the glia pool contracts have not been audited. Bugs, exploits, or design flaws could lock or drain funds with no recourse.

Dependencies

A keeper process and the pool balance decide when compute is available. The website can be down while the chain keeps running, and the chain can be congested while the site looks fine.

Inference

Model output can be wrong, outdated, or made up. Nothing a model says on this site is advice of any kind.

No advice

Nothing on this site is financial, legal, or tax advice. We do not recommend buying, selling, or holding any token.

Do your own research

Read the contracts, check the treasury and pool balances on the block explorer, and verify every claim yourself before acting on it.
